About

Who Am I

I am a common man from Odisha (known as the Holy Land of Lord Jagannath).

I am basically a technical graduate who completed B.E. in the stream of Electronics and Telecommunication at my native place.

I have been seeing so many ups and downs in my life regarding my career, job and other challenges after 2005.

Finally, I was working as a Project Engineer in a private company. My company helped me in every way to balance my situation. Here we were working for NTPC Barh, NTPC Barunei and Neo Carbons. But these works were project-oriented, which meant location instability.

My Rescue Journey

So I am professionally an Engineer but personally a compassionate rescuer and caregiver for needy living beings who are in pain in front of my sight.

Basically, I am living an ethical and environmental “Vegan” and “Teetotaler” lifestyle at the age of 15, without knowing these two words I mentioned.

From my childhood I started rescuing and caregiving street-roaming abandoned cows, male calves, poor street dogs and injured monkeys, etc. Sometimes old abandoned roadside sick people too. And all these works were spot-on treatment in general. I never ever wanted fostering and providing shelter like other rescuers do.

I keep on serving living beings, especially animals in most scenarios, in Angul in Odisha, Parel and Thane in Mumbai, HSR Layout in Bangalore, Safilguda in Secunderabad, Old Gajuwaka in Visakhapatnam and finally here in Barh in Bihar. By GOD's mercy, everywhere some people helped me like good companions.

In this journey I experienced so many tragic cases at the time of Lockdown, which taught me about street animals' lifestyles. In some cases I totally lost faith in NGOs and Rescue Centers, but have little faith in GOD and good deeds.

My Current Situation

In Barh, I just came to complete a project, but life placed helpless beings in my path. Faced with the choice to ignore their suffering or respond with compassion, I chose humanity.

Since 2020, I have been providing shelter, food, and medical care to abandoned, injured, old, and disabled street dogs. Whenever possible, I also help mentally challenged, injured, abandoned, and elderly people in my locality. What began as helping a few helpless souls has become a lifelong responsibility.

For over five years, I managed everything from my salary. Today I am unemployed, yet 25 rescued dogs inside my shelter and around 45 street dogs outside depend on me every single day. I have not returned to my native place for more than six years because these rescued dogs know only me and wait for me.

Future

Mission

  • Live life with Compassion — Make kindness a practical responsibility, not only an idea.
  • Saving life of Living beings who are in pain in your close proximity area or locality at least — Stand beside animals who need care, protection and a safe future.
  • Try to Protect the Nature — Respect the natural world and the many forms of life within it.
  • Heal the Planet — Choose everyday actions that reduce harm and encourage responsible living.

Vision

I want to plant the seed of compassion in everyone. I want them to care not just about the whole world, but feel responsible for helping those living in pain around them.

I want them to help both humans, animals and birds. If nothing else, at least give encouragement to people like us.

“Make compassion a practical responsibility.”
